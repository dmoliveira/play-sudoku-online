🧭 How to play · 流れ

How Suguru differs

▾
  • Each cage uses 1 up to its size. Small cages resolve fast; larger cages carry the main logic.
  • Matching digits cannot touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • The board, number pad, and live cage chip already mirror the active cage range so you can stay focused on deduction instead of memorizing limits.
🎮 Modes & rituals · 型

Daily, challenge, and assist rhythm

▾
  • Classic is the easiest way to learn cage reading while keeping notes and checks flexible.
  • Daily keeps one deterministic board per date and level.
  • Challenge lowers assist feedback so you rely more on cage reading and neighbor pressure.
  • No mistakes and No notes are lighter ways to increase pressure without changing the puzzle set.
